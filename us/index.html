Meet the team

Names, titles and bios as they appear on the current Killeen site.

Chief Executive Officer

R. Matthew George, D.C.

At our practice, we are committed to providing the highest quality medical and chiropractic care. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact me directly at drgeorge@anthonychiro.com.

Dr. George earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology from Marshall University in West Virginia in 2006. He continued his education at Parker College of Chiropractic, where he earned a second bachelor's degree in Anatomy and his Doctorate in Chiropractic Care in 2010.

In 2012, Dr. George joined Anthony Medical & Chiropractic Center and has grown with the company ever since. Today, he serves as an owning provider and the Chief Executive Officer, dedicated to enhancing patient care and outcomes.

A passionate advocate for health and wellness, Dr. George enjoys staying active with trail running and participating in community races and events. He also finds joy in golfing with family and friends. In addition to chiropractic care, Dr. George has a strong interest in computer science and is committed to leveraging technology to improve healthcare delivery and patient well-being.

When he's not working, Dr. George is a proud family man who treasures quality time with his wife and two children, creating lasting memories together. Outside of work and family, he has a deep love for concerts and live music, always seeking the next opportunity to enjoy the vibrant energy of a great performance.

Chiropractor

Jake Diamond, D.C.

Born in Dallas and raised in the Hill Country town of Kingsland, TX, Dr. Jake Diamond brings a deep-rooted Texas spirit and a strong commitment to health and wellness to his chiropractic practice.

He earned both his Doctor of Chiropractic degree and a Bachelor of Science in Anatomy from Parker University in 2024. Currently, Dr. Diamond is furthering his expertise by pursuing a Master of Business Administration with a major in Health Care Management.

Dr. Diamond is passionate about delivering high-quality care that supports the body's natural ability to heal. He is dedicated to ongoing professional growth and refining his techniques to provide the best outcomes for his patients.

When he's not in the clinic, Dr. Diamond enjoys staying active, whether he's working on his golf game, weightlifting, exploring the outdoors, or checking out new places with friends. He's also a fan of pickleball and axe throwing.

Chiropractor

Jason Rounds, D.C.

Dr. Jason Rounds brings a unique background and a deeply personal understanding of pain, recovery, and long-term spinal health to his patients.

Before becoming a chiropractor, Dr. Rounds owned and operated a residential construction company, Onsite Design and Construction, where he built homes in Maine. During one winter project, a serious job-site accident changed the course of his life. While working on a two-story structure in icy conditions, unsecured staging shifted, causing Dr. Rounds to fall two stories. The impact resulted in herniated discs at L4 and L5, leaving him with significant pain and limited options.

After consulting with multiple medical providers who recommended spinal fusion surgery, Dr. Rounds sought a more conservative approach. A massage therapist recognized structural issues in his lower spine and referred him to a chiropractor. Through imaging and examination, the underlying cause of his injuries became clear. After his very first chiropractic adjustment, Dr. Rounds experienced noticeable relief. Over the course of care, his improvement was so profound that it inspired a complete career change.

At the age of 40, Dr. Rounds made the decision to pursue chiropractic medicine, enrolling at Logan University in St. Louis, Missouri. He graduated in 2016 and has since spent over nine years practicing chiropractic care in multiple clinical settings as an intern and associate doctor. Throughout his career, he has developed experience with a wide range of chiropractic techniques, believing that effective care comes from choosing the right approach for each individual patient, sometimes requiring a firm correction, other times a gentle, precise adjustment.

Dr. Rounds is passionate about helping patients find relief, restore function, and improve their overall quality of life. He looks forward to working with you and helping you achieve the same meaningful improvement in health that led him to chiropractic care.

Chiropractor

Trinh Nguyen, D.C., M.B.A.

Dr. Nguyen is a proud Louisiana Tech alum, where she earned her Bachelor's degree in Kinesiology and Health Sciences. She continued her education at Parker University, earning both her Doctor of Chiropractic and Master of Business Administration.

She is passionate about providing patient-centered care that helps patients move better, function better, and work toward long-term health and wellness.

Dr. Nguyen is committed to continued learning and providing compassionate, high-quality care tailored to each patient's individual needs. She believes that addressing the muscles and other soft tissues is just as important as adjusting the joints and incorporates both into her approach to help patients move and function at their best. She is passionate about working alongside her patients to better understand their goals and develop a personalized approach to care.

Outside of the clinic, Dr. Nguyen enjoys staying active at the gym, exploring the shops around the Killeen-Temple area, discovering new local restaurants, and spending time with her partner and their three dogs.

Chiropractor

Kameren Jelks, D.C.

Born in Waco, Texas, and raised in Dallas, Dr. Kameren Jelks brings his Texas roots, passion for health and wellness, and dedication to patient care to Anthony Medical & Chiropractic Center.

Acupuncturist

Dr. James

Dr. James has been practicing Chinese Medicine and acupuncture since 2003 in Taiwan and has been practicing in Texas since 2012. He the 4th generation in his family to practice Chinese medicine, spanning over 150 years of passing down experience and knowledge!

Dr. James graduated from Texas Health and Science University in 2012. During his studies, he had the most clinical hours of practice and was known as the student in practice with the most patient recommendations. Most of his patients show significant improvement after the first treatment session.

Dr. James is specializes in nerve damage, such as neuropathy, pinched nerve, trigeminal neuralgia, shingles, sciatica, carpal tunnel, abnormal tingling and numbness sensation; also in internal medicine, such as circulation, stress, digestive dysfunction, female issue, infertility, low energy, obesity, fibromyalgia.

Not only does Dr. James inherit the full knowledge of the most ancient medical science in the world but he's continued his education of modern health information in order to better provide treatment to his patients. It is his philosophy that the treatment is more than a “treatment.” The goal of his practice is to give patients a healthier and better life quality in the most natural way, such as through acupuncture, herbal medicine and balance nutrition.

Nurse Practitioner

Andrea “Cristi” Flynn, APRN, FNP-C

Andrea “Cristi” Flynn, APRN, FNP-C is a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner and U.S. Army veteran with over two decades of experience in nursing and healthcare leadership.

She earned her Bachelor of Science in Nursing from the University of Louisville in 2003 before commissioning into the U.S. Army Nurse Corps, where she served honorably as an operating room nurse in both overseas and domestic assignments.

Following her military service, Cristi continued her work in perioperative care and labor and delivery, cultivating a strong passion for patient education. In 2019, she completed her Master of Science in Nursing and Family Nurse Practitioner program, later dedicating her practice to veterans' healthcare.

Cristi and her active-duty husband settled in Central Texas in 2017 with their two daughters. Together, they run a small horse farm in Belton, focusing on the conservation and responsible breeding of endangered Irish Draught horses for dressage, jumping, and vaulting. Deeply committed to community engagement and sharing her passion for the sport, Cristi teaches equestrian riding and vaulting lessons and regularly coaches her team at competitions. She integrates her holistic approach to wellness into her teaching, helping students develop strength, confidence, and balance both in and out of the saddle. She also enjoys traveling and making memories with her husband and daughters.

Physician Assistant

MAJ (Ret.) James S. Lorenz, MPAS, PA-C

MAJ (Ret.) James Lorenz is a board-certified Physician Assistant with over two decades of dedicated service in both military and medical settings. He earned his Bachelor's degree in Physician Assistant Studies from Midwestern University in 2006 and went on to complete his Master's degree in Physician Assistant Studies at the University of Nebraska in 2010.

James proudly served in the United States Army for 23 years, retiring in 2022 with the rank of Major. His military career shaped his clinical expertise, leadership, and commitment to providing patient-centered, high-quality care in diverse and often challenging environments.

Today, James brings that same level of dedication and discipline to his civilian practice. He is passionate about improving patients' health through evidence-based medicine, compassionate care, and a strong provider-patient relationship.

Outside of work, James enjoys spending time outdoors fishing and hiking, as well as staying active through weight lifting. He values balance in life and strives to model the healthy lifestyle he encourages in his patients.
